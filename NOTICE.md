## Version continuity

This fork continues the upstream `2.0.0` line. The first release on this fork
is `3.0.0`, which raises the minimum deployment target to iOS 15. Per
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/), dropping platform support is
treated as a backward-incompatible change, hence the major version bump.

Version history is shared with upstream up to and including `2.0.0`; releases
from `3.0.0` onward are maintained here.
